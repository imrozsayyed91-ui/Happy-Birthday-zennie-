Happy Birthday meri zennie 💗🥺🎂

Meri cutu, meri jaan, meri sabse pyaari zennie… aaj ka din mere liye bhi special hai, kyunki aaj us insaan ka birthday hai jo mere dil ke bahut kareeb hai. Kaash main tumhare paas hota, tumhe ek tight hug deta, tumhari cute si smile dekhta aur sabse pehle tumhe birthday wish karta. 🫂💗

Pata hai, tumhari chhoti-chhoti baatein bhi mere din ko beautiful bana deti hain. Tumhara hansna, tumhara cute sa gussa, tumhari baatein aur tumhara woh pyaara sa andaaz — ye sab mere liye bahut precious hain. Kabhi-kabhi shayad main apni feelings achhe se express nahi kar pata, lekin iska matlab ye nahi ki tum mere liye special nahi ho. Tum ho, bahut zyada ho. 🥹💕

Meri zennie, meri dil se dua hai ki tumhari life khushiyon se bhar jaaye. Tumhare saare dreams poore hon, tumhe har woh cheez mile jo tumhara dil chahta hai, aur tumhare chehre ki smile kabhi kam na ho. Jab bhi life difficult lage, yaad rakhna ki tumhe har waqt strong banne ki zaroorat nahi. Tumhari feelings matter karti hain, tumhara sukoon matter karta hai, aur tum jaisi ho waise hi precious ho. 🌷

Main chahta hoon ki hum sirf happy moments hi nahi, difficult moments mein bhi ek doosre ko samjhein. Hum ek doosre ki respect karein, honestly baat karein, galtiyon se seekhein aur saath grow karein. Mere liye pyaar sirf cute messages nahi, balki care, patience, trust aur ek doosre ko samajhne ki koshish bhi hai. 💞

Kaash main tumhe bata pata ki tumhari smile dekhkar mujhe kitni khushi hoti hai. Agar kabhi tum khud ko less samjho, toh yaad rakhna ki tumhare andar bahut saari beautiful qualities hain. Apne dreams kabhi mat chhodna, apna khayal rakhna, achhe se khana aur rest bhi karna, meri cutu. Tumhari khushi mere liye important hai. 🫶

Aaj tumhare birthday par meri wish hai ki tumhe peace mile, beautiful memories milen, achhe log milen aur itna pyaar mile ki tumhara dil khush ho jaaye. Kaash ye little website tumhare face par ek cute si smile la sake. Ye meri taraf se tumhare liye ek virtual hug aur ek chhota sa reminder hai ki tum bahut special ho. 🎀

Happy Birthday once again, meri jaan. Thank you for being you. May this new year of your life bring you endless happiness, success, love and beautiful surprises. I hope tum hamesha apni cute si smile ke saath khush raho. 💗

I love you, meri zennie. Aaj ka din tumhara hai, aur ye chhota sa surprise bhi sirf tumhare liye. 🥺💗
